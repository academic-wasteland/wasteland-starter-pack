Register your town’s services and resources

Publish your own JSON-LD catalogue, start/restart your worker, then register from a second terminal:

python3 -m wasteland --state .town fair-publish catalogue.jsonld
# Keep your town worker running in another terminal:
python3 -m wasteland --state .town fair-register

Registration validates your authenticated town’s catalogue and returns a revision receipt. Town repository and registration guide. Descriptions are public metadata; access remains controlled by each provider.
